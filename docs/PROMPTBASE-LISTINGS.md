Editable variables included:
[PRODUCT], [BRAND_TYPE], [STYLE], [SCENE], [PALETTE]

### Tags
product photography, ecommerce, ad creative, hero banner, branding, chatgpt image, commercial, marketing visuals

Use high-end commercial product photography quality with clean materials, realistic reflections, balanced contrast, and strong subject separation.
Composition should keep clear negative space for ad text and remain readable at social and landing-page crop ratios.
Output should look like a high-end commercial ad campaign, ultra detailed, clean, polished, realistic materials, premium reflections, no clutter, no watermark, no logo distortion.
